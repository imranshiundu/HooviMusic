# Hoovi Music

**Hoovi Music** is a personal music vault and offline player.

It is designed to import playlist metadata, organize a user-owned music library, match tracks against safe and permitted sources, and keep music playable offline.

Hoovi Music is not a Spotify clone, stream-ripping tool, or piracy downloader. Spotify-style playlist import should be treated as metadata import only: song title, artist, album, artwork references, playlist structure, and user library organization.

## Product position

Hoovi Music helps users keep their music close.

Core promise:

> Import your playlists. Match your music safely. Play your library offline.

## User-facing rules

- Do not say “download Spotify songs.”
- Do not say “free Spotify.”
- Do not imply the app bypasses DRM, subscriptions, or platform restrictions.
- Say “import playlist metadata.”
- Say “match tracks from user-owned, licensed, public-domain, Creative Commons, or otherwise permitted sources.”
- Say “offline playback for tracks you own or are allowed to store.”

## Early feature direction

- Playlist metadata import
- Local music library scanner
- Offline playback
- Music matching engine
- Safe source resolver
- Lyrics and metadata manager
- Downloads manager for permitted sources only
- Desktop-first player UI
- Future mobile support

## Brand language

Use calm, premium, direct language.

Recommended lines:

- Hoovi Music keeps your playlists alive.
- Your music library, rebuilt safely.
- Import, match, keep, play.
- Offline music without the chaos.
- A personal music vault for the songs you care about.

Avoid:

- Free Spotify
- Spotify downloader
- Stream ripper
- Unlimited free music
- Download any song
