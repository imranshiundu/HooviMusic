> Hoovi Music imports playlist metadata, matches tracks from safe sources, and keeps your allowed library playable offline.

## Import screen

Title:

> Import playlist metadata

Subtitle:

> Connect a music account or upload a playlist file. Hoovi Music imports titles, artists, albums, artwork references, and playlist order. It does not download protected streams.

CTA:

> Start import

## Match screen

Title:

> Match your library safely

Subtitle:

> Hoovi Music checks your local files and permitted sources to find playable versions of your tracks.

CTA:

> Start safe match

States:

- Matched
- Needs review
- Blocked
- Local file found
- Offline ready
- Source not allowed

## Offline screen

Title:

> Offline library

Subtitle:

Empty state:

> No offline tracks yet. Save allowed tracks from your matches or scan your local library.

## Downloads screen

Title:

> Downloads

## About copy

Hoovi Music is a personal music vault and offline player. It imports playlist metadata, organizes your music library, and helps match tracks from safe, permitted sources.

Hoovi Music does not download protected streams or bypass platform restrictions.
