# Hoovi Music Brand Guide

## Name

Primary name: **Hoovi Music**

Short name: **Hoovi** only when the context already makes music clear.

Internal codename option: **Tunza**

Do not use:

- Spotube
- Spotify clone
- Spotify downloader
- Stream ripper
- Free Spotify

## One-line description

Hoovi Music is a personal music vault that imports playlist metadata, safely matches tracks, and keeps your allowed library playable offline.

## Product promise

Import your playlists. Match your music safely. Play your library offline.

## Tone

Hoovi Music should sound:

- premium
- calm
- useful
- direct
- legally careful
- not childish
- not overhyped

## Safe copy examples

Use:

- Import playlist metadata
- Match tracks safely
- Keep allowed tracks offline
- Scan your local library
- Rebuild your music collection
- Play your library anywhere
- Find legal sources
- Save tracks you own or are allowed to store

Avoid:

- Download any song
- Download Spotify music
- Bypass restrictions
- Stream ripping
- Free premium music
- Unlimited downloads
- Clone Spotify

## App sections

Recommended user-facing sections:

- Home
- Library
- Playlists
- Imports
- Matches
- Offline
- Downloads
- Local Files
- Lyrics
- Settings

## Empty states

### No playlists imported

Import playlist metadata to start rebuilding your library.

### No offline tracks

Tracks you own or are allowed to store will appear here after they are saved offline.

### No matches yet

Hoovi Music has not matched this playlist yet. Start a safe match scan to look through your local files and permitted sources.

### Source blocked

This source cannot be used for offline storage. Hoovi Music only saves tracks from sources that allow it.

## Legal-facing product rule

Spotify and other streaming services may be used only for metadata import when allowed by their terms. Hoovi Music must not download protected streams, bypass DRM, or market itself as a way to avoid subscriptions.
