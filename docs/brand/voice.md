# Hoovi Music voice

Hoovi Music should sound calm, premium, and clear.

No hype. No fake rebellion. No childish jokes. No scary legal walls in the main flow.

## Voice pillars

### 1. Honest

Say what the app can do and what it cannot do.

Use:

> This source does not allow offline storage.

Avoid:

> Something went wrong.

### 2. Fast

Write short interface copy. Users are trying to listen, not read a contract.

Use:

> Review match

Avoid:

> We found a possible track that may or may not correspond to your selected imported metadata item.

### 3. Library-first

Hoovi Music is about keeping a user's library clean.

Use:

> Found in your files

Avoid:

> Grabbed from the internet

### 4. Premium

Use quiet confidence.

Use:

> Keep your music close.

Avoid:

> The ultimate insane music downloader.

## Core vocabulary

Use these words:

- import
- metadata
- library
- match
- review
- source
- permitted
- offline
- local files
- confidence
- playback
- storage

Avoid these words in user-facing copy:

- rip
- clone
- bypass
- unlimited
- exploit
- free premium
- grab everything

## Buttons

Good buttons:

- Import playlists
- Scan local files
- Start safe match
- Review matches
- Save offline
- Open source details
- Locate file
- Clear cache

Bad buttons:

- Download all
- Rip playlist
- Bypass
- Force download

## Error copy

### Source limited

> This source allows playback but not offline storage.

### File missing

> The file moved or was deleted. Locate it again to restore playback.

### Match uncertain

> This looks close, but Hoovi Music needs you to review it first.

### No match

> No safe match yet. Try scanning your local files or checking another permitted source.

## Empty states

### Home

> Import a playlist or scan your music folder to begin.

### Matches

> No matches yet. Start a safe match scan to compare your playlists with local files and permitted sources.

### Offline

> No offline tracks yet. Saved tracks will appear here when their source allows offline storage.

## Taglines

Primary:

> Import your playlists. Match your music safely. Play offline.

Secondary:

- Keep your music close.
- Your playlists, preserved.
- Offline music without the chaos.
- A personal vault for the songs you care about.
